Fight news

The announcement —
turning professional

After eight years, 70+ amateur fights, and six London titles — the decision has been made. Here's what turning professional means, what comes next, and why now is the right time.

[F]
Mitchell Asare
70+ Amateur fights
6× London Champion
8 Years competing

Why now

The honest answer is that I've been ready for a while. The amateur game gave me everything — structure, discipline, the foundation of everything I know about boxing. But there's a ceiling. And I've been standing at that ceiling long enough to know it's time to go through it.

Turning professional isn't a step away from what I've built. It's the next chapter of the same story. The same work ethic, the same commitment, the same hunger — just in a different arena now.

"There's a ceiling in the amateur game. I've been standing at it long enough to know it's time to go through it." — Mitchell Asare

What comes next

The debut is confirmed. The date is set. The venue is booked. Over the coming weeks I'll be sharing more about the preparation — the training camp, the weight, the mindset going into fight night — right here on this blog.

If you've supported me through the amateur years, this is what it's been building to. If you're new here — welcome. You've arrived at the right moment.

Get your tickets

Tickets for the debut are available now. The venue has limited capacity and I want to see as many familiar faces as possible in that crowd on fight night. The link is at the top of every page on this site.

More soon. Train hard. Stay sharp.
