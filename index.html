The fighter

Made through discipline.
Born for this.

Mitchell Asare has spent eight years earning his place in professional boxing; not through luck but through relentless work, discipline and an amateur career that speaks for itself. Six London titles. An international silver medal. An Elite ABA Final.

"You have to love boxing — because once you’re in that ring, you’re fighting for your life."

Now, turning professional, he steps into a new chapter. Everything that came before was training. This is the beginning.
